I am currently a postdoctoral fellow in the Mathematics and Computer Science Division at Argonne National Laboratory, working on `federated learning`, `distributed optimization`, `privacy-aware machine learning`, and `ML for science`.
My work combines algorithm design, statistical heterogeneity, and practical evaluation, with an emphasis on methods that remain useful under realistic system and data constraints.

I received my Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from [UNIST](https://www.unist.ac.kr) in August 2024.
My recent work spans personalized federated learning, welfare-aware optimization, and machine learning applications in health and scientific domains.
I am currently exploring both `industrial research scientist` and `assistant professor` opportunities.
