AAggFF asks how federated learning should optimize when the goal is not only average performance, but overall welfare across participants.
The project was published at ICML 2024 as *Pursuing Overall Welfare in Federated Learning through Sequential Decision Making*.

## Why It Matters

- Standard federated objectives can hide uneven outcomes across clients
- Sequential optimization offers a principled way to balance system-wide welfare
- This line of work connects online decision making with collaborative machine learning
