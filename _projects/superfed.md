SuPerFed studies personalized federated learning through the geometry of low-loss solutions.
The project was published at KDD 2022 as *Connecting Low-Loss Subspace for Personalized Federated Learning*.

## Why It Matters

- Personalized federated learning should adapt to user-specific data rather than forcing a single global optimum
- The method connects optimization structure with personalization quality
- The project represents my broader interest in federated learning under statistical heterogeneity
